# Northwind Systems — Employee Onboarding Policy

_Fictional sample document for testing. Not a real company._

## §1. Purpose

This policy defines the steps required to onboard new employees at Northwind
Systems, including account provisioning and required training.

## §4. Security Awareness Training

§4.1 Security is everyone's responsibility. All personnel are expected to
understand the company's information security practices.

§4.2 All new hires must complete the Security Awareness course through the LMS
within their first 30 days of employment. Completion is tracked by People Ops
and is a condition of continued system access.

## §5. Account Provisioning

§5.1 IT provisions accounts on the employee's start date based on their role.
