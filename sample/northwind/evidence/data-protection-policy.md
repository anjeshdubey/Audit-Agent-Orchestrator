# Northwind Systems — Data Protection Policy

_Fictional sample document for testing. Not a real company._

## §1. Encryption in Transit

§1.1 All data transmitted over public networks is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or
higher.

## §2. Encryption at Rest

§2.1 Sensitive customer data is protected using encryption.

## §3. Vulnerability Management

§3.1 Production systems are monitored for known vulnerabilities, and identified
issues are remediated by the engineering team.

§3.2 Automated vulnerability scans are performed on at least a quarterly basis.
