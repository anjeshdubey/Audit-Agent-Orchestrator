# Northwind Systems — Access Control Policy

_Fictional sample document for testing. Not a real company._

## §2. Access Reviews

§2.1 Management reviews user access to production systems periodically to
confirm that access remains appropriate for each user's role.

§2.2 Access that is no longer required is revoked following the review.

## §3. Authentication

§3.1 Multi-factor authentication is required for all access to production
systems.

## §4. Termination

§4.1 Upon termination of employment, IT revokes the departing user's access to
all systems within 24 hours of the effective end date.
